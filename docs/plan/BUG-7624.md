# BUG-7624 — Text hidden under minimap/scrollbar

**Status:** TODO
**Type:** BUG
**Branch:** `bugfix/bug-7624-text-under-minimap`
**Run:** bugfix/2026-10-23-minimap-preferences-fonts

## Objective

Nothing at the right or bottom edge of an editor may cover its text. That covers the minimap, the
vertical scrollbar when *Settings → Minimap* is off, and the horizontal scrollbar. It must hold when a
scrollbar grows under the pointer, at any font size, with word wrap on or off.

## Context & constraints

- `Views/EditorPaneView.axaml`:
  - row 1 is a `*,Auto` grid: the AvaloniaEdit `TextEditor` in column 0, the `EditorMinimap`
    (36 px, `App.axaml` control theme) in column 1;
  - the editor's `VerticalScrollBarVisibility` is `Hidden` with the minimap and `Auto` without;
  - `HorizontalScrollBarVisibility` is `Auto`.
- The editor scrolls through its template's `PART_ScrollViewer`, which `EditorPaneView` already finds
  in `OnEditorTemplateApplied` and drives for the minimap.
- That scroll viewer keeps the Fluent default `AllowAutoHide=True`. In that mode the content presenter
  spans the scrollbar column and row: the bars float over the text and widen when the pointer is on
  them ("when going big it hides a part of the text").
- The tab strip (`ListBox#Tabs`) and the help dialog already turn `AllowAutoHide` off for the same
  reason.
- Existing headless tests: `tests/…Desktop.UnitTests/Views/EditorMinimapTests.cs` (`Show(harness)`
  helper, real `MainWindow`).

## Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|
| The editor's scroll viewer gets `AllowAutoHide=False`: its scrollbars are laid out beside the text, in their own column and row, and are always full size | Removes the covering at its cause. A bar that floats over the content and widens on hover always covers it. House precedent: the tab strip, the help dialog |
| A small gutter between the text and whatever stands at its right (the minimap, or the vertical scrollbar), in the editor's background | The last column no longer touches the strip beside it, which reads as cut off even when it is not covered |
| Applied in the pane's code-behind where `PART_ScrollViewer` is found (or a `/template/` style on the pane's editor), not in `App.axaml` | Only the editor needs it; other scroll viewers keep their look |
| Reproduced first by a headless layout test, then fixed | "The text can be hidden" has to be pinned to a measurable overlap before it is called fixed |

## Steps

1. Reproduce in a headless test (real `MainWindow`, `EditorHarness`):
   - a document with a line far wider than the pane, and more lines than fit;
   - scroll to the far right and to the bottom;
   - measure the text view's visible area in window coordinates against the minimap's bounds, the
     vertical scrollbar's bounds (minimap off) and the horizontal scrollbar's bounds;
   - also check that the end of the longest line, scrolled into view, is fully left of the
     minimap/scrollbar.
2. `EditorPaneView`: turn off `AllowAutoHide` on the editor's scroll viewer, and add the gutter
   (e.g. a left margin on the minimap and a right margin inside the scroll viewer's content, whichever
   keeps the map's and the bar's geometry tests valid).
3. Keep the minimap's maths working: viewport fractions use the scroll viewer's viewport, which now
   excludes the bars.
4. Tests:
   - with the minimap: the text area never overlaps the minimap, and the bottom line is not under
     the horizontal scrollbar;
   - with the scrollbar: the text area never overlaps the vertical scrollbar, wrapped or not;
   - at a large font size (e.g. 40), the end of the longest line, scrolled into view, is left of the
     strip;
   - the existing minimap tests stay green.

## Acceptance criteria

- With the minimap, no character is drawn under it, at any font size, wrapped or not.
- With *Settings → Minimap* off, no character is drawn under the vertical scrollbar, including while
  the pointer is on it.
- The last visible line is never under the horizontal scrollbar.
- A visible gap separates the text from the minimap or scrollbar.
- Zero warnings, whole suite green.

## Out of scope

- Restyling the scrollbars themselves (width, colours, arrows).
- The hex view's own scrolling.

## Decisions taken autonomously

| Question | Chosen | Why | Alternatives rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| How the text is kept clear | Bars beside the text (`AllowAutoHide=False`) + a gutter | Fixes the cause for both bars and the minimap in one rule, and the app already does this elsewhere | Right padding sized to the widened bar only (still covered at the bottom, and tied to a theme metric); an overlaid, translucent minimap |
| Scope of the change | The pane's editor only | The draft names the editor text | Every scroll viewer in the app |
| Gutter size | A few pixels (≈ 6), fixed | Enough to separate the text from the strip; a font-relative gap is not needed | No gap (text still touches the strip); a gap that grows with the font |
| Proof | Headless layout test of bounds | Measurable and kept as a regression test | Visual check only |
