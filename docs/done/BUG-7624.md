# BUG-7624 — Text hidden under minimap/scrollbar

**Item:** BUG-7624 — Text hidden under minimap/scrollbar
**Branch:** `bugfix/bug-7624-text-under-minimap`
**Run:** bugfix/2026-10-23-minimap-preferences-fonts

## Summary

The editor's text is no longer drawn under its scrollbars, and it never runs against the minimap or the
vertical scrollbar.

A headless probe of the real window (1200 × 800, a 600-character line, 200 lines) measured the bug
before the fix:

- **Minimap off:** the text area ran under the vertical scrollbar. Scrolled fully right, the long line
  ended at x = 1196, under the bar at 1183–1199. With word wrap on, every wrapped row ran 16 px under
  the bar too. The bar widens under the pointer, so it covered more there ("when going big").
- **Both modes:** the bottom 16 px of the text area were under the horizontal scrollbar, so the last
  line on screen was half covered.
- **Minimap on:** the text ran right up to the minimap (text area 1–1163, map from 1163). A caret past
  the end of the longest line was cut 5 px short at the map's edge.

The cause is the Fluent `ScrollViewer` default `AllowAutoHide=True`: it lays its content under both
bars and lets them float over it.

**The fix** is in `EditorPaneView.axaml`:

- A `/template/` style turns `AllowAutoHide` off on the editor's `PART_ScrollViewer`. The bars get their
  own column and row beside the text, and stay at full size. The tab strip and the help dialog already
  do this.
- `Padding="0,0,6,0"` on the editor leaves a 6 px gap between the text area and whichever strip stands
  beside it. The text area clips to its own bounds, so the text stops short of the strip.

After the fix, the probe measured:

- the text area ends 6 px before the minimap or the vertical bar;
- it stops above the horizontal bar;
- the end of the longest line, scrolled fully right, sits inside it, at 14 and at 40 px, wrapped or
  not.

## Decisions taken at build time

| Question | Chosen | Why | Alternatives rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where the gutter lives | `Padding` on the `TextEditor`, which its template hands to the scroll viewer's content presenter | One setting gives the gap in both modes: between the text and the minimap, and between the text and the vertical bar. The probe showed the text area clips to itself, so the padding stays empty | A left margin on the minimap (no gap in scrollbar mode); a margin on the bar through `/template/` (two rules for one gap) |
| How auto-hide is turned off | A `/template/` style in the pane's `UserControl.Styles` | Declarative, scoped to this pane's editor, and in force before the template is applied | Setting it in `OnEditorTemplateApplied` (code-behind for a style concern) |
| Gutter size | 6 px | Clearly separates the text from the 36 px map and the 16 px bar at every size measured | A gap that grows with the font (not needed: the gap separates, it does not reserve room for glyphs) |
| Caret past the end of the longest line | Left as AvaloniaEdit has it (3 px of slack past the widest line) | Vim's normal mode keeps the block caret on a character, and insert mode's thin caret fits in the slack. Only a block caret forced past the end could be cut, now at the text area's edge rather than at the map's | Widening the text view's extent (AvaloniaEdit internals) |

## Deviations & follow-ups

- **Step 2 of the plan offered two places for the gutter.** The padding inside the scroll viewer was
  chosen (see above); the minimap's geometry and its tests are unchanged.
- **The minimap's viewport now excludes the horizontal bar** when that bar shows (686 instead of
  702 px in the probe), because the scroll viewer's viewport does. The window it draws is therefore
  exact. The existing minimap tests pass unchanged.
- **Visible change:** with *Settings → Minimap* off, the vertical bar is always drawn at full width
  instead of as a thin line that widens on hover. The same goes for the horizontal bar whenever a line
  is wider than the pane.
- **Fix budget:** none used. The first full build and test run after the change were green.
- **Line endings:** no line-ending noise in this dev's diff; nothing to recommend.
